📄 Research Publication

Three Phase Transmission Line Fault Detection using Arduino

◈ IJRASET · Volume 12 · May 2024

Published a research article in the International Journal for Research in Applied Science & Engineering Technology (IJRASET), under the guidance of Mr. Pankaj Kumar Mahto. The paper presents an Arduino-based system to detect single line-to-ground, double line, and double line-to-ground faults in three-phase transmission lines — reducing fault correction time and improving power supply continuity.
